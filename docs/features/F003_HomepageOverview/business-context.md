## Why It Matters

The homepage is the front door of SAA 2025 — it has to answer "what is this and when does it start" in seconds, keep the current award programme visible, and point people toward more detail or the recognition campaign, without forcing anyone to log in first.

## Who Uses It

- **Any visitor (no login required)** — lands here first, checks the countdown and event details, decides whether to look at the awards.
- **Sunners who already know the programme** — use the award cards as a shortcut straight to their category of interest on the award-system page.

## What They Do

1. Visitor opens the homepage and reads the hero — theme, live countdown, and event time/venue.
2. Visitor scrolls past the Root Further introduction to the awards section.
3. Visitor picks an award category card and is carried to that category's detail on the award-system page.
4. Visitor notices the Kudos promo block and can look at it later — the Kudos board itself is not open yet.
