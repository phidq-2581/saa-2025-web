## Why It Matters

Sunners want a single place to see the full picture of the SAA 2025 award programme — which categories exist, how many people or teams win each, and what each is worth — that they can jump to directly from the header link or a category card on the Homepage.

## Who Uses It

- **Sunner (any signed-in employee)** — browses the six award categories to understand what they could be recognized for, and sees the Sun* Kudos promotion at the end of the page.

## What They Do

1. The Sunner opens the award page from the header's "Awards Information" link, or from a Homepage award card — the second path drops them straight onto the matching category instead of the top of the page.
2. The Sunner reads the hero banner and section title introducing the SAA 2025 award programme.
3. The Sunner picks a category from the left-hand list — the page scrolls to that category and highlights the choice so they always know where they are.
4. The Sunner reviews each award's photo, description, headcount, and prize value across all six categories.
5. The Sunner notices the Sun* Kudos promotion at the end of the page; the "see details" button is visible but does not go anywhere yet — that programme's own page is not ready this round.
