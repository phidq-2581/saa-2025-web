## Screen List

| Screen Name | SCR### | What User Sees | What User Can Do |
|-------------|--------|-----------------|-------------------|
| Hệ thống giải | SCR006_AwardSystem | Keyvisual hero, section title, a 6-item left category menu, six award info cards, and a Sun* Kudos promo block (header/footer/FAB are owned by F002_NavigationShell) | Click a category to scroll to it and see it highlighted; scroll through all six award cards; see (not use yet) the Kudos "Chi tiết" button |

## User Journey

1. Sunner arrives at Hệ thống giải — either from the header link (lands at the top) or a Homepage award card (lands already scrolled to the matching category).
2. Sunner sees the hero banner and the section title, then scans the left category menu.
3. Sunner clicks a category in the menu — the page scrolls to that award and the menu highlights the chosen category, replacing whichever was highlighted before.
4. Sunner reads each award card's image, description, headcount, and prize as they scroll.
5. Sunner reaches the Sun* Kudos promo block at the end of Hệ thống giải and sees its "Chi tiết" button, which does not navigate anywhere yet.
